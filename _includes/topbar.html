{%- comment -%} A bar, not the off-canvas sidebar it replaces. Below the nav breakpoint that sidebar started closed, so a visitor arriving from the printed QR code — a phone, in a poster hall, under a minute — saw no navigation at all until they found a toggle they had no reason to look for. That is why the homepage needed a poster banner bolted on to it. This shows every destination at every width, and took ~90 lines of off-canvas CSS and a ~40-line toggle state machine with it: the site now ships no navigation JavaScript at all. It WRAPS rather than scrolls or collapses. A link on a second row is still a link; a link behind a hamburger, or off the end of a horizontal scroll strip, is invisible — which is the failure being fixed here. {%- endcomment -%}
