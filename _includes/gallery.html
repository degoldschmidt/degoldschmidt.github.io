That one is a linked figure with a title and a "more" affordance; this one is a media mount whose job is to hold a video at a known aspect ratio and caption it. Sharing a class between them would mean every future change to one had to be checked against the other. NO AUTOPLAY, deliberately. The printed QR code points at the site ROOT, so the homepage is the page that lands in a poster hall on conference wifi — three autoplaying loops is megabytes spent before the visitor has decided they care, and it is the one place on the site where that cost is least affordable. `preload="none"` means the poster still is all that downloads until someone taps. It also keeps the site honest about `prefers-reduced-motion`. CSS cannot pause a video, so an autoplaying loop could only respect that setting with JavaScript — and the navigation JS was just deleted.
