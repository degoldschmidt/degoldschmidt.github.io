Outreach

I believe that scientific outreach is key to making research accessible, fostering public trust, and inspiring people from diverse backgrounds to engage with science. Communicating our findings beyond the scientific community is not only a responsibility but also an opportunity to demonstrate how research can enrich society and address real-world challenges.
